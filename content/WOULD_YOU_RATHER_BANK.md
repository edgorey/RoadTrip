# Would You Rather Bank

These are unscored discussion prompts. Ask for a reason only when the group is engaged; there is no correct answer. All entries are `reviewed`.

## Adventure and imagination

- **WYR-ADV-001:** Explore the deepest ocean in a submarine, or travel around the Moon in a spacecraft?
- **WYR-ADV-002:** Discover a hidden ancient city, or discover a completely new animal species?
- **WYR-ADV-003:** Be able to breathe underwater, or fly safely through the air?
- **WYR-ADV-004:** Spend a night in a museum after everything comes alive, or in a friendly haunted castle?
- **WYR-ADV-005:** Have a map that shows hidden places, or a compass that points toward whatever you most need?

## Knowledge and abilities

- **WYR-SKILL-001:** Instantly speak every human language, or play every musical instrument?
- **WYR-SKILL-002:** Remember everything you read, or learn any physical skill twice as fast?
- **WYR-SKILL-003:** Ask one question about the past and get a true answer, or see one event 100 years in the future?
- **WYR-SKILL-004:** Be brilliant at solving mysteries, or brilliant at inventing things?
- **WYR-SKILL-005:** Have a perfect sense of direction, or always know exactly what time it is?

## Travel and nature

- **WYR-TRAVEL-001:** Visit every country once, or return to your favorite country every year?
- **WYR-TRAVEL-002:** Live for a month in a treehouse, or on a houseboat?
- **WYR-TRAVEL-003:** Watch a volcanic eruption from a completely safe observatory, or see the northern lights from a glass cabin?
- **WYR-TRAVEL-004:** Hike through a rainforest with an expert guide, or cross a desert in a comfortable expedition vehicle?
- **WYR-TRAVEL-005:** Travel everywhere by train, or everywhere by boat?

## Playful choices

- **WYR-FUN-001:** Have a friendly miniature dragon, or a friendly robot dog?
- **WYR-FUN-002:** Only be able to whisper for a day, or only be able to sing your words?
- **WYR-FUN-003:** Live in a world made of building bricks, or a world drawn like a cartoon?
- **WYR-FUN-004:** Always win at board games, or always know the perfect joke?
- **WYR-FUN-005:** Design a new sport, or direct a new adventure film?

## Host notes

Avoid turning preferences into personality diagnoses. Do not pressure a player to defend a choice. Generate follow-ups from the stated reason, not from stereotypes.
