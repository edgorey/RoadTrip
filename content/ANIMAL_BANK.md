# Guess the Animal Bank

Read one clue at a time. Earlier guesses earn no extra points by default; avoiding bonus scoring reduces ambiguity. All entries are `reviewed`.

## ANIMAL-LAND-001 — Elephant — easy

1. I am a very large land mammal.
2. I have wide ears and thick legs.
3. I use a long trunk to breathe, smell, drink, and grasp.
- **Accept:** elephant, African elephant, Asian elephant

## ANIMAL-OCEAN-001 — Octopus — easy

1. I live in the ocean.
2. I have no bones and can squeeze through narrow gaps.
3. I have eight arms.
- **Accept:** octopus

## ANIMAL-LAND-002 — Cheetah — easy

1. I am a spotted cat.
2. I live mainly in Africa.
3. I am the fastest land animal over short distances.
- **Accept:** cheetah

## ANIMAL-POLAR-001 — Polar bear — easy

1. I am a large predator of the Arctic.
2. My skin is dark beneath translucent-looking fur.
3. I often hunt seals on sea ice.
- **Accept:** polar bear

## ANIMAL-OCEAN-002 — Blue whale — easy

1. I am a mammal that lives in the ocean.
2. I feed largely on tiny krill.
3. I am the largest animal known to have lived.
- **Accept:** blue whale

## ANIMAL-FOREST-001 — Sloth — medium

1. I live in trees in Central and South America.
2. Algae may grow in my fur.
3. I am famous for moving very slowly.
- **Accept:** sloth

## ANIMAL-OCEAN-003 — Narwhal — medium

1. I am an Arctic whale.
2. I travel in groups and use echolocation.
3. Many males have a long spiral tusk.
- **Accept:** narwhal

## ANIMAL-AIR-001 — Peregrine falcon — medium

1. I am a bird of prey found across much of the world.
2. I hunt other birds.
3. During a hunting dive, I am the fastest animal.
- **Accept:** peregrine falcon, peregrine

## ANIMAL-WETLAND-001 — Axolotl — hard

1. I am native to lakes and canals near Mexico City.
2. I retain juvenile aquatic traits as an adult.
3. I am a salamander known for regenerating limbs.
- **Accept:** axolotl

## ANIMAL-OCEAN-004 — Mantis shrimp — hard

1. I am a marine crustacean.
2. My complex eyes can detect kinds of light humans cannot.
3. Some of my species strike prey with extremely fast club-like limbs.
- **Accept:** mantis shrimp

## ANIMAL-DESERT-001 — Fennec fox — medium

1. I am a small mammal of the Sahara and nearby deserts.
2. I am mostly active at night.
3. My unusually large ears help release heat.
- **Accept:** fennec fox, fennec

## ANIMAL-AUS-001 — Platypus — medium

1. I am native to eastern Australia.
2. I am a mammal that lays eggs.
3. I have a broad bill and webbed feet.
- **Accept:** platypus, duck-billed platypus
