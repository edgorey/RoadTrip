## Host rules

- Do not change the target after play begins.
- Keep a count of actual yes/no questions, not side comments.
- If a compound question contains two different tests, ask the player to split it.
- If classification is genuinely conditional, answer “sometimes” and give only the minimum clarification.
- Reveal the target at 20 questions or when the group gives up.
