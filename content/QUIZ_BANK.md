# Quiz Bank

All seed items have status `reviewed` and review date 2026-09-30. Difficulty is a starting point; adapt it per player. Ask the prompt only; keep answers, aliases, and notes internal until ruling.

## Geography

### QUIZ-GEO-001 — easy

- **Prompt:** What is the capital of Portugal?
- **Answer:** Lisbon
- **Accept:** Lisboa
- **Note:** Lisbon is Portugal’s capital and largest city.

### QUIZ-GEO-002 — easy

- **Prompt:** Which is the largest ocean on Earth?
- **Answer:** Pacific Ocean
- **Accept:** the Pacific
- **Note:** The Pacific is larger than all Earth’s land area combined.

### QUIZ-GEO-003 — medium

- **Prompt:** Which river flows through the cities of Porto and Vila Nova de Gaia?
- **Answer:** Douro
- **Accept:** Douro River, Rio Douro
- **Note:** The Douro reaches the Atlantic between the two cities.

### QUIZ-GEO-004 — medium

- **Prompt:** On which continent is the Sahara Desert?
- **Answer:** Africa
- **Accept:** none
- **Note:** The Sahara spans much of North Africa.

### QUIZ-GEO-005 — hard

- **Prompt:** What imaginary line at zero degrees longitude passes through Greenwich?
- **Answer:** Prime Meridian
- **Accept:** Greenwich Meridian, meridian of Greenwich
- **Note:** It is the reference line for longitude.

## History

### QUIZ-HIST-001 — easy

- **Prompt:** Which Portuguese navigator reached India by sea in 1498?
- **Answer:** Vasco da Gama
- **Accept:** Vasco da Gama
- **Note:** His fleet reached Calicut, now Kozhikode.

### QUIZ-HIST-002 — easy

- **Prompt:** Which ancient civilization built the pyramids at Giza?
- **Answer:** Ancient Egyptians
- **Accept:** Egyptians, ancient Egypt
- **Note:** The major pyramids were royal tombs of Egypt’s Old Kingdom.

### QUIZ-HIST-003 — medium

- **Prompt:** In which city was the Roman Colosseum built?
- **Answer:** Rome
- **Accept:** Roma
- **Note:** Construction began under Emperor Vespasian.

### QUIZ-HIST-004 — medium

- **Prompt:** What name is given to the invention that used movable type to reproduce books in fifteenth-century Europe?
- **Answer:** Printing press
- **Accept:** movable-type printing press
- **Note:** Johannes Gutenberg is closely associated with its European development.

### QUIZ-HIST-005 — hard

- **Prompt:** Which treaty, signed in 1494, divided newly encountered lands outside Europe between Portugal and Castile?
- **Answer:** Treaty of Tordesillas
- **Accept:** Tordesillas
- **Note:** The division used a meridian west of the Cape Verde islands.

## Science and nature

### QUIZ-SCI-001 — easy

- **Prompt:** What gas do humans need to breathe to survive?
- **Answer:** Oxygen
- **Accept:** O2
- **Note:** Cells use oxygen in aerobic respiration.

### QUIZ-SCI-002 — easy

- **Prompt:** What force pulls objects toward Earth?
- **Answer:** Gravity
- **Accept:** gravitational force
- **Note:** Gravity attracts masses to one another.

### QUIZ-SCI-003 — medium

- **Prompt:** What process do plants use to turn light energy into chemical energy?
- **Answer:** Photosynthesis
- **Accept:** none
- **Note:** Plants use light, water, and carbon dioxide to produce sugars.

### QUIZ-SCI-004 — medium

- **Prompt:** What is the name of molten rock after it reaches Earth’s surface?
- **Answer:** Lava
- **Accept:** none
- **Note:** Underground molten rock is called magma.

### QUIZ-SCI-005 — hard

- **Prompt:** What part of a cell contains most of its genetic material in plants and animals?
- **Answer:** Nucleus
- **Accept:** cell nucleus
- **Note:** Mitochondria also contain a small amount of DNA.

## Natural hazards

### QUIZ-EARTH-001 — easy

- **Prompt:** What instrument records vibrations caused by earthquakes?
- **Answer:** Seismograph
- **Accept:** seismometer
- **Note:** Seismometers measure ground motion; seismographs record it.

### QUIZ-EARTH-002 — easy

- **Prompt:** What rotating tropical storm is called a hurricane in the North Atlantic?
- **Answer:** Tropical cyclone
- **Accept:** cyclone
- **Note:** Regional names include hurricane and typhoon.

### QUIZ-EARTH-003 — medium

- **Prompt:** What is the point inside Earth where an earthquake begins called?
- **Answer:** Focus
- **Accept:** hypocenter, hypocentre
- **Note:** The epicenter is the point directly above it at the surface.

### QUIZ-EARTH-004 — medium

- **Prompt:** What giant sea wave can be triggered by an undersea earthquake?
- **Answer:** Tsunami
- **Accept:** none
- **Note:** Landslides and volcanic activity can also generate tsunamis.

### QUIZ-EARTH-005 — hard

- **Prompt:** Which scale expresses an earthquake’s magnitude using seismic moment?
- **Answer:** Moment magnitude scale
- **Accept:** Mw, moment magnitude
- **Note:** It has largely replaced the Richter scale for large earthquakes.
