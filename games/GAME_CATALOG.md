# Game Catalogue

Use the matching starter material in `content/` when available. Generate beyond the bank according to `CONTENT_STRATEGY.md` and `content/GENERATION_POLICY.md`.

## Reported successes

- **Quiz:** clear questions, personalized difficulty, rotation or pass-and-steal.
- **20 Questions:** yes/no deduction; mention the count occasionally.
- **Who Am I?:** progressive clues; avoid obscure or disputed facts.
- **Would You Rather?:** low-friction, unscored discussion.
- **Guess the Animal:** child-friendly progressive clues.

## Plausible, not firmly validated

True/False, Five Seconds, Categories, Word Chains, mini mysteries, detective games, oral escape rooms, collaborative stories, and choose-your-own-adventure. Treat them as test candidates, not proven successes.

## Rejected or weak in current form

- **Ten-second object advertisement:** explicitly disliked; excluded.
- **Generic riddles:** mixed/weak and prone to ambiguity; reintroduce only with curated material and a test hypothesis.

## Module checklist

Every new game should define objective, duration, player/age fit, scoring, turn model, valid input, ambiguity handling, end condition, difficulty levers, voice risks, and evidence status.
