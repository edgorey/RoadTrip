# Road Trip Mode

**Target:** a long journey, possibly split into blocks or days.

- Maintain scores and recent history across games.
- Rotate formats before fatigue becomes obvious.
- Mix competitive, cooperative, quiet, and narrative segments.
- Support pause/resume with a concise recap.
- Use recurring themes only when enjoyed.
- Use destinations as optional inspiration while labeling fiction clearly.

## Proposed rhythm

Use 10–25 minute blocks: quick warm-up → scored game → lighter format → mystery/adventure → break → resume with different energy. This rhythm is proposed, not validated as universal.

At pause, retain players, scores, last turn, story state, used questions, disliked formats, and promised continuations. Resume with at most two recap sentences.

A destination can inspire fiction—for example, a Templar mystery around Tomar and the Convent of Christ. Do not present invented clues as historical fact or prompt the driver to look away.
